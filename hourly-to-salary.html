Introduction to Hourly Wages and Annual Salaries

Comparing job offers, setting freelance consulting rates, or qualifying for a residential lease often requires translating an hourly wage into an equivalent annual salary. While hourly workers are compensated strictly for the hours logged on a timesheet, salaried employees receive a fixed annual sum divided across regular pay periods. Bridging the two formats lets you evaluate total compensation on a level playing field.

What Is an Hourly to Salary Calculator?

An hourly to salary calculator converts an hourly pay rate into weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, and annual gross earnings based on the number of hours worked per week and paid weeks worked per year. Our workspace also includes optional fields for time-and-a-half (1.5×) overtime hours and an estimated effective tax rate so you can preview both gross compensation and approximate take-home pay.

Important note: Core salary conversions estimate gross earnings before income taxes, payroll withholdings, health insurance premiums, or retirement contributions. The optional effective tax rate field provides a general net estimate, not an official payroll tax calculation.

How to Convert Hourly Pay Into Annual Salary

For a standard full-time schedule of 40 hours per week across 52 paid weeks per year (2,080 total hours), multiply your hourly wage by hours per week and then by weeks per year:

Annual Gross Salary = Hourly Wage × Hours per Week × Weeks per Year

At $42.00 per hour for 40 hours a week over 52 weeks, your gross annual salary is $42.00 × 40 × 52 = $87,360.00.

How to Calculate Weekly Earnings

Weekly gross pay is the foundation of every longer timeframe. Multiply your base hourly rate by your regular weekly hours. If you work overtime compensated at 1.5× your base rate, add the overtime pay to your regular weekly pay:

Weekly Gross Pay = (Hourly Rate × Regular Hours) + (Hourly Rate × 1.5 × Overtime Hours)

How to Calculate Monthly Income

A common mistake when estimating monthly income is multiplying weekly pay by 4. Because only February (in non-leap years) has exactly 28 days, a full 52-week year averages 4.333 weeks per month (52 ÷ 12). Always calculate monthly gross income by dividing your annual gross salary by 12:

Monthly Gross Income = Annual Gross Salary ÷ 12

Once you know your monthly take-home pay, you can feed that figure directly into the 50/30/20 Budget Splitter to organize your monthly expenses.

Understanding Gross Annual Income

Gross annual income is the total pre-tax compensation your employer or clients pay you over a 12-month period. Lenders use gross income to compute debt-to-income (DTI) ratios when you apply for financing (which you can model in our Simple Loan Calculator). However, household budgets should always be built on net take-home pay after federal, state, and payroll deductions.

How Working Hours Affect Your Salary

Small differences in weekly hours or paid time off significantly alter annual income. Reducing a schedule from 40 hours to 37.5 hours per week (a 7.5-hour workday with an unpaid 30-minute lunch) cuts annual paid hours from 2,080 to 1,950—reducing a $30/hour worker's annual gross income from $62,400 to $58,500.

What Happens When You Work Part-Time?

Part-time workers and students typically log 15 to 30 hours per week and may take unpaid weeks off during school terms or holidays. Entering your exact weekly hours (for example, 24 hours) and paid weeks (for example, 48 weeks) produces a realistic annual projection instead of an inflated 2,080-hour estimate.

Overtime, Unpaid Leave and Variable Working Hours

Hourly non-exempt workers often earn 1.5× their regular rate for hours worked beyond 40 in a workweek. Even 5 hours of weekly overtime at $30/hour adds $225 per week—or $11,700 per year across 52 weeks. Conversely, contractors and freelancers who take 4 weeks of unpaid vacation and holidays work 48 billable weeks per year; adjusting the "Paid Working Weeks" input to 48 immediately reflects the true annual revenue.

Realistic Salary Conversion Examples

  • $20/Hour Full-Time (40 hrs × 52 wks): Equals $800/week, $1,600 bi-weekly, $3,466.67/month, and $41,600.00/year gross.
  • $35/Hour Contractor (40 hrs × 48 billable wks): Equals $1,400/week worked, averaging $5,600.00/month and $67,200.00/year gross after accounting for 4 unpaid weeks off.
  • $42/Hour with 5 Weekly Overtime Hours (52 wks): Regular pay ($1,680/wk) plus 5 OT hours at $63/hr ($315/wk) equals $1,995/week, or $103,740.00/year gross.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many working hours are in a standard full-time year?

A standard full-time schedule of 40 hours per week across 52 weeks equals exactly 2,080 paid hours per year (including paid holidays and paid time off).

Why is monthly income not equal to weekly pay times four?

Four weeks equal 28 days, whereas 11 out of 12 months have 30 or 31 days. Across 52 weeks, the average month contains 4.333 weeks (52 ÷ 12). Multiplying weekly pay by 4 understates your annual income by four full weeks of pay.

Does this calculator deduct federal or state income taxes?

The primary annual salary figure represents gross pre-tax income. We also provide an optional Estimated Effective Tax Rate (%) field so you can approximate net take-home pay across all pay frequencies.

How do I convert an annual salary back into an hourly wage?

Divide your annual gross salary by the total number of paid hours worked per year (typically 2,080). For example, $72,800 ÷ 2,080 hours = $35.00 per hour.

How should freelancers use the Paid Weeks per Year field?

Freelancers and independent contractors do not receive paid vacation, sick days, or paid federal holidays. Subtract your planned vacation and holiday weeks from 52 (for example, enter 46 or 48 weeks) to see your realistic annual billable income.
